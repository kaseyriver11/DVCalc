## Open findings, ranked

### Must fix before the next owner-facing push

1. **Raw error strings reach owners.** `account.html` uses `alert()` with Supabase messages (add year, delete account); "Couldn't save: {error}" on My Contracts and Bookings; the email-code dialog passes Supabase's rate-limit text through; `badges_info.html` shows repo paths on failure. One fixed sentence per failure, detail to `console.error`, map the known RPC messages ("Balances changed", "Not enough current points") to owner copy. See the copy audit, finding 5.
2. **Failed reads still masquerade as empty data on three pages:** calendar contracts/itineraries ("Just browsing"), Saved Itineraries ("No saved itineraries yet"), Compare Itineraries ("You need at least 2"). `DVCAuth.readFailed()` exists and Home/My Contracts/Bookings use it; these three don't.
3. **Two "is the window open" tests disagree inside one calendar card.** `currentBookingWindowKey()` counts months as days/30.44; `monthsFromTodayCutoff()` uses calendar months. Today with check-in Apr 25, 2027 the outlook chip treats 7 months as open while the eligibility line says it opens tomorrow. `dvc-leftover-points.bookingWindowKey` has the same divisor. Make both calendar-month based (the outlook bucket can stay "nearest of 11/7/5/3/1" but computed from the calendar-month opening dates).
4. **Membership Value 0% when stays lack version-2 point sources.** Deliberate (`dvc-trip-funding.js` never trusts older metadata), but an owner whose stays were all recorded before the breakdown existed sees 0% paid back and three "Review point sources" rows with no bulk path. Offer "Attribute all to {contract}" for single-contract owners, or pre-fill the review form from `contract_id`.
5. **Contract Value mixes bases inside one metric:** Cost/Pt/Year deflates dues by value growth (real terms) while Lifetime Outlay and breakeven use nominal dues, so `costPerPointYear × points × years` never equals the row's outlay (SSR 20 yrs: $8.41 vs $13.68 average dues per point). Pick one basis and say which.
6. **Copy conventions were set two weeks ago and the older pages were never swept.** Title Case buttons and labels on the calendar, Buying Guide, Suggest a Stay, Compare, Membership Value, My Contracts modals; "trip"/"log" vocabulary across `trips.html`, `dvc-badges.js` (40+ strings), `dvc-trip-funding.js`; ~200 ASCII "--" dashes; four names for entering a balance ("Add points left", "Confirm balance", "Add balance", "Review balance"); three for the Disney check ("Reconcile with Disney", "Check balance with Disney", "Check your Disney balance"); four for use-year cycles ("Current Year/Plan Ahead", "This/Next use year", "Current/Next", "Now/Next"). One mechanical pass. Details in the copy audit sections 9, 12, 13, 15, 16.

### Should fix

7. **Compare Resorts' "My points vs renting" column is the same 54% on every row** (dues ÷ rental price is constant when the dues resort is fixed). The dollar figure varies only with points. Drop the percent or replace the column with something that differs per row (points per night, or cash $/pt).
8. **"#1 Best Value" in Compare is whichever row is first under the current sort.** Under Points ascending it's the cheapest, not the best value. Tie the badge to a fixed metric or drop it when sorting by anything else.
9. **Studio category mixes sleeps-2 Duo/Tower studios with sleeps-4/5 studios** in Compare's ranking. Suggest a Stay has a party-size filter; Compare doesn't.
10. **Booking Outlook abbreviations "Ex / Fr / Lo / NL"** on the calendar's 5-cell strip are opaque on a phone with no legend nearby; the tooltip exists but isn't discoverable.
11. **The stacked wallet on My Contracts hides each covered card's deadline pill** (the "Bank by" pill sits at the bottom of the card, exactly where the next card overlaps it, and bleeds through as ghost text). Put the pill in the always-visible header band, or show "Use 40 by Nov 30" on a card past its banking deadline (today it shows nothing).
12. **Accessibility basics:** calendar day cells, wallet cards, wizard steps, `.alt-card`, segment edit/remove spans are divs with click handlers and no role/keyboard path (zero `role="button"` in the repo); modals don't move focus or trap Tab; no `aria-pressed` on most chip groups; icon-only prev/next buttons unlabeled; unescaped `itin.name` in four `innerHTML` sites (a nickname with `&` renders wrong). Copy audit 20 to 24.
13. **Native `confirm()`/`prompt()`/`alert()`** for delete contract, remove year, delete account, delete itinerary, and four validation alerts; Bookings already has the styled danger dialog to reuse.
14. **Hand-built date formatters** remain in ~20 places (`app.js` `formatDisplayDate` drops the year on window-opening dates that routinely fall in a different year than check-in; `use-points.html` "Tue 9/29"; `changes.html` "Sep 20–Sep 26"; `contractvalue.html` long form). Load `dvc-dates.js` on compare/changes/contractvalue/trips and route through it.
15. **Waitlist Whisperer badge** names a feature removed 2026-09-23 (it counts tricky itineraries). Rename the label; the id can stay.
16. **`paymentsMadeBy("2025-01-31","2025-02-28")` returns 0** in `dvc-financing.js`: a loan started on the 31st gets its February payment counted on March 1. Off by one payment for month-end start dates.
17. **Inclusive year counts:** Lifer and Points Steward use `currentYear - startYear + 1`, and `dvc-owner-value.js` charges a full year of dues for the purchase year, so a contract bought Dec 15, 2025 shows "2 years owned" on Jan 1, 2026. Points Steward's numerator also sums future-year rows against a denominator that stops at the current year.
18. **Online check-in milestone** in Saved Itineraries' .ics is 30 days out; Disney's online check-in opens 60 days out. The 11-month .ics event fires for every segment regardless of home resort.
19. **`yearsRemaining = expirationYear - currentYear`** in Buying Guide counts 16 years for a 2042 deed that expires Jan 31, 2042 (15 usable use years).

### Missing features owners ask for, ranked by how often they came up

1. **Contract-size planner with per-contract closing math** ("buy 300 now or 150 twice?"): $500 administration fee + $300-500 title + estoppel per closing. Buying Guide step 2. Effort M.
2. **Expiring-points rescue via an existing booking.** Owners with current points about to hit the banking deadline and an upcoming booking 31+ days out can re-modify the booking with no changes; the online system redraws banked/borrowed first and frees current points to bank. Suggest it from the bankable point action when such a booking exists. Effort S. https://www.disboards.com/threads/expiring-points.3983505/
3. **Resale listing evaluator inputs**: loaded vs stripped premium $5-15/pt, $500 fee, $150 estoppel, 6.9-10% commission, dues reimbursement on banked points, ROFR point freeze. Effort M.
4. **Sell-side net proceeds** with the OKW 2042/2057 split and a commission picker (DVC Sales 6.9%, most brokers 9-10%; `dvc-resale.js` uses 10% + $150). Effort S.
5. **Transfer tracking (no pricing).** Six 2026 DISboards threads on transfer rules. A `transferred` bucket that keeps its own home resort and use year, counted against one transfer in or out per use year. Effort M.
6. **Cross-use-year stays**: note that online they become two reservations and only Member Services can merge them. The app already splits nights by use year. Effort S.
7. **Calendar export (.ics)** of banking, use-by and Holding deadlines. DVC Toolkit and dvchelp.com both do it. Effort S.
8. **Sorcerer AP via Membership Magic Beyond preset** in Worth the Points? (75 pts + $99 ≈ $15.60/pt). Effort S.
9. **"Hardest to book at 7 months" ranked view** from the availability data the app already ships. Effort S.
10. Direct-incentive-aware Buying Guide (S); fixed-week flag on a contract (S, low priority); forwarded-confirmation-email import (L, only if booking entry proves to be friction).

Deliberately not proposed (boundary or prior decision): availability alerts, waitlists, pricing transfers, ROFR %.

### What the app does that no competitor found does

Four-bucket ledger with Holding, per contract and use year, with a Disney check and immutable activity history; cancellation and Holding outcomes computed from Disney's terms; Smart Draw, multi-contract split and the swap simulator; Booking Outlook, Suggest a Stay and leftover-points tied to the owner's recorded balances and windows; Buying Guide across 17 resorts with compounding dues and real deed dates; Membership Value on actual historical dues; 2016 to 2027 charts in one calendar; Worth the Points?; deadline emails that name the actual recorded points. Competitors compared: DVC Toolkit, DVC Trip Planner, dvchelp.com, DVC Field Guide, PointHoppers, DVC Fan and DVC Resale Market calculators, DVC Sales resale value calculator, dvcrofr.com, dvcspreadsheets.com, David's and DVC Rental Store.

## Copy audit summary

Nav structure and labels are identical on all 14 pages and test-enforced; no stale product or page names remain in user-visible text; the two $/pt kinds are wired correctly everywhere; the "never connects to Disney" line holds on every surface; `DVCPointAttention.describe()` is genuinely shared. The newest surfaces (Bookings & Stays, Use These Points, Worth the Points?, Point actions, reminder emails) follow the conventions. The older, larger surfaces don't, per open finding 6. Page `<title>`s use five patterns and three `<h1>`s disagree with their nav label ("DVC Points Calendar" vs Calendar, "Resort Comparison" vs Compare Resorts, "DVC Membership Value" vs Membership Value); no page has a meta description. "House Money" is never defined for a signed-in member. Buying Guide's mobile cards have no "?" for Cash Ratio / Breakeven Horizon. Membership Extras is promised unconditionally on the Direct card (150-point minimum applies).
